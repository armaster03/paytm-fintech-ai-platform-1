# Paytm Crypto Insights: Blockchain and Financial Risk Note

## 1. Governance, Stablecoin Risk and DeFi/DAO Controls

When Paytm Money offers any crypto or blockchain-related product to users it must have a strong governance framework in place. The first thing to consider is selection. Stablecoins that are backed by cash or government securities are less risky because we can see what is backing them. They are easy to sell. On the hand algorithmic stablecoins rely on complex mechanisms that can fail under stress. For example the Terra/Luna collapse happened because the mechanisms in place failed when a lot of people wanted to withdraw their money at the time. For an app like Paytm Money stablecoins backed by cash are the safest choice.

The second thing to consider is DeFi and DAO governance risk. Any token that Paytm Money recommends must be carefully checked for governance concentration, key structure, smart-contract audit status and exposure to liquidity manipulation or flash-loan attacks. Tokens with much power in the hands of a few people, weak protections or unclear governance upgrades are riskier for regular users. In practice Paytm Money should only recommend assets with reporting, clear governance processes and credible audit trails. Without these safeguards Paytm Money may expose users to protocol failure and a false sense of security.

## 2. Crypto Asset Class Recommendation for Retail Advisory

From a portfolio theory perspective cryptocurrencies do not fit well into an investment portfolio. They do not generate income do not pay dividends and their value is not based on any underlying economic stream. This means that traditional investment theories do not support holding crypto as a long-term investment for users. Additionally crypto returns can be unpredictable and volatile with correlation to traditional assets and high risk of failure.

For Paytm Moneys advisory product the safest recommendation is to allocate zero percentage to crypto. A small speculative exposure may be suitable for net-worth users who understand the risk but even then the allocation should be very low. A reasonable recommendation is to set a maximum of 1% to 2% of a portfolio for speculative crypto exposure but only with strong investor disclosure and risk acknowledgment. For users a zero-allocation recommendation is more suitable because the product prioritizes capital preservation, clarity and trust over speculation.

## 3. T.A.N.G. Fraud Framework and Bank-Side Defenses

The T.A.N.G. Framework is especially relevant to a platform like Paytm Money because fraudsters often exploit urgency and trust.

### Greed Vector

This vector refers to scams that promise high returns or "guaranteed wealth" through crypto or investment platforms. Fraudsters often pose as advisors or trading agents pushing users to transfer funds quickly through instant payments. This is highly relevant for Paytm Money because the platform is already involved in money movement, credit and digital wealth.

- Bank-side defense: Paytm Money should use a real-time transaction risk engine with controls on transactions, verification of new payees and friction on high-risk payments. Any sudden spike in transfer volume to beneficiaries should trigger a hold or biometric challenge before execution.

### Authority Vector

This vector involves impersonating regulators, tax agencies, police or bank officials to pressure users into making payments. In practice scammers threaten account freezes tax penalties or compliance measures unless the user immediately settles a "or transfers funds.

- Bank-side defense: Paytm Money should use real-time language scanning to detect spoofing patterns, coercive language and known scam signatures. This should be paired with account-linking detection to flag users who receive messages and initiate high-risk transfers for verification or blocking.

These controls reduce the effectiveness of social-engineering fraud by slowing down transactions and forcing verification before critical money movement occurs. For a payments and lending platform, like Paytm Money this is essential to maintain trust reduce fraud losses and protect customers from financial harm and reputational damage.
