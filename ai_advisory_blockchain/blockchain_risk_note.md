# Paytm Crypto Insights: Blockchain & Financial Risk Note

## 1. Governance & Stablecoin Safeguards
To offer a retail-facing watchlist, Paytm Money must mandate strict disclosures around stablecoin pegging mechanisms and DeFi governance structures. Fiat-collateralized stablecoins backed 1:1 by cash or short-dated government securities present minimal de-pegging risk compared to algorithmic stablecoins, which rely on endogenous feedback loops susceptible to death spirals (e.g., Terra/Luna). For DeFi protocol tokens, risk scoring must evaluate governance token concentration, admin-key multi-sig thresholds, smart-contract audit histories, and flash-loan vulnerability parameters before presenting tracking metrics to retail users.

## 2. Crypto Asset Class Recommendation
Under CAPM and modern portfolio theory, cryptocurrencies do not constitute a core asset class due to their lack of underlying cash flows, intrinsic value, or yield generation. While low historical correlation with equities and high positive skewness are frequently cited by proponents, these characteristics are heavily confounded by survivorship bias, extreme volatility, fat-tailed tail-risk, and high operational/transaction costs. Therefore, for Paytm Money’s retail advisory product, we recommend a **0% allocation baseline**, with an absolute cap of **1% to 2%** strictly limited to aggressive, high-net-worth investor profiles seeking speculative exposure.

## 3. T.A.N.G. Fraud Framework & Bank-Side Defenses

*   **Greed Vector (Yield/Investment Scams):** Fraudsters impersonate wealth advisors, promising guaranteed high returns via crypto trading platforms to trick users into initiating instant UPI/wallet transfers.
    *   *Bank-Side Defense:* Real-time risk scoring engine implementing outbound velocity controls, step-up biometric/mPIN verification, and dynamic beneficiary-friction holds on high-risk dynamic QR payments to newly added VPAs.
*   **Authority Vector (Fake Regulatory/Tax Demands):** Scammers spoof law enforcement or tax authorities, threatening dynamic freeze of Paytm accounts unless immediate penalty settlements are transferred via payment gateways.
    *   *Bank-Side Defense:* Real-time NLP deep-packet inspection of transaction narration text paired with inbound account-linking pattern detection to auto-block transactions matching known spoofing signatures.
